Interact
  • Hold the switch or slider thumb: it swells into a clear lens that magnifies the track. Drag it, then let go.
  • Drag across the segmented control: the indicator turns into a droplet.
  • Press the buttons: glass grows and glows at the touch point.
  • The … button morphs into a menu; the refraction map is rebuilt every frame as it changes shape.
