Every control here is plain markup plus LiquidGlass.init() from dist/. No page-specific glass code.

  • Hold a switch or slider thumb: it swells into a magnifying lens.
  • Drag across the segmented control or tab bar: the selection becomes a droplet.
  • Press buttons: they grow and glow from the touch point.
  • The … menu morphs open; its map rebuilds as it changes shape.
